He specializes in building scalable microservices with Spring Boot, designing RESTful APIs, optimizing PostgreSQL relational databases, implementing Redis distributed caching, building responsive React.js frontends, and automating cloud infrastructure on AWS.

Aditya currently works as a Software Engineer II at CGM ARIA Health Services as part of the CGM APEasy Team, focusing on healthcare software products. He is certified as an AWS Certified Developer – Associate, AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner, and GitHub Foundations credential holder.

Aditya is actively expanding his technical domain into AI engineering in Java, leveraging Spring AI to integrate Large Language Models (LLMs) and intelligent capabilities into enterprise backend services.
